Welcome. This space will carry short, practical notes on changes in tax and corporate law, written for business owners, professionals and individual taxpayers.

## What you will find here

- **Income Tax:** return filing, deductions, the old and new regimes, TDS and notices.
- **Direct Tax:** amendments, CBDT circulars and notifications, and important judicial rulings.
- **Companies Act:** MCA amendments, ROC compliance, and due dates for companies and LLPs.

Each post explains what changed, who it affects and what to do next, with examples where they help.
